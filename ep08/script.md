# EP08 — "GABAN" (3 VS 3)

**Format:** vertical 9:16, ±2.5–3 minutes, about 26 shots of 5–10 s (one Higgsfield clip per shot).
**Language:** the English dialogue/captions go on screen (global audience, same as the trailer). The Indonesian translation is in brackets for the team only and doesn't go on screen.
**Cast:** Pom-Pom, Jarik, GABAN · Jimothy, GERALD, NUGGET · (the owner, at the end).

**Timeline of the day:**

| Act | Time | Location |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | just before dawn (dark, cool blue) | balcony/terrace → living room |
| 2 | first light of dawn | living room (Pom-Pom & Jarik trapped) |

## ACT 3 — GABAN arrives (sunrise, from the east)

**Shot 11 — The sun rises**
- **Visual:** outside, the garden is still grey-blue. From the east, the first rays break over the garden wall and the sky turns orange. The raccoons squint at the light (they are nocturnal), and GERALD covers his eyes.
- **Audio:** the music goes quiet, leaving only wind and the flutter of birds.

**Shot 12 — The silhouette (the hero shot)**
- **Visual:** on top of the eastern wall, directly in front of the sun, the **silhouette of a huge cat** stands up. The long body comes first, then the plume tail **rises slowly until it towers above his ears**, with its tip curling forward. The sun behind him makes the edge of his fur glow gold.
- **Camera:** slow-motion, low angle, upward.

**Shot 13 — The call of the land**
- **Visual:** GABAN raises his head and lets out **a deep, long, echoing Maine Coon yowl**, like a roar. Leaves fall, birds scatter, and the pond ripples.
- **SFX:** *"MRRRROOOOWWWWRRR!"*
- **Caption:** `THIS IS MY TERRITORY NOW.`

**Shot 14 — The raccoons' reaction**
- **Visual:**
  - Jimothy's pie falls out of his mouth.
  - GERALD's toothpick drops.
  - NUGGET's eyes go wide, sparkling: "…Cousin?!"
  - GABAN slow-blinks: *"Brrrp… nope."*

**Shot 15 — The descent (recreating the reference video)**
- **Visual:** GABAN jumps down from the wall and lands softly on his big snowshoe paws. Then comes the **slow-motion walk towards the camera**: head low and steady, tail swaying high, fur shining in the sun (recreating `Karakter_Maine_coon_GABAN.mp4`).
- **Caption:** `GABAN — Maine Coon. Pom-Pom's childhood friend.`

**Shot 16 — Relief**
- **Visual:** inside the living room, Pom-Pom and Jarik press their faces to the French door glass. Pom-Pom smiles wide with tears in his eyes, and Jarik throws up both arms.
- **Pom-Pom:** "GABAAAN!"
- **Visual:** the raccoons, who were inside, panic and grab their loot. GERALD opens the terrace door and all three **run out into the garden** to escape. They are now outside, on GABAN's ground.

## ACT 4 — The plan and the 3 VS 3 battle (garden and pond, golden morning)

**Shot 17 — The huddle**
- **Visual:** behind a bush in the garden, GABAN, Pom-Pom and Jarik huddle together. GABAN scratches a plan into the soil with his claw: three stick-figure raccoons, three arrows, and **a circle for the pond**.
- **GABAN (calm, rural Maine accent, very few words):** "Raccoons love water. Get 'em to the pond. Ayuh." *(Rakun suka air. Giring mereka ke kolam. Ayuh [= iya].)*
- **GABAN (pointing one by one):** "Tall one's mine. Round one's yours, Pom. Little one…" **Jarik (cracking his knuckles):** "…is MINE." *(Yang tinggi punyaku. Yang bulat punyamu, Pom. Yang kecil… — …punyaku.)*
- **Visual:** the three of them put their paws together.
- **SFX:** GABAN gives a short chirp: *"Mrrt!"* (the attack signal).

**Shot 18 — The face-off (the cover shot)**
- **Visual:** a wide shot of the green lawn beside the pond, with the sun low behind the heroes. **Left:** GABAN, Pom-Pom, Jarik. **Right:** GERALD, Jimothy, NUGGET. Each pair faces off at matching heights: **GABAN ↔ GERALD, POM-POM ↔ JIMOTHY, JARIK ↔ NUGGET**. The wind blows falling leaves between them.
- **Caption:** `3 VS 3`

**Shot 19 — Duel 1: JARIK vs NUGGET (the pond edge)**
- **Visual:**
  - Jarik rolls **Pom-Pom's stolen gold bell** along the pond edge. It jingles and shines in the sun.
  - NUGGET can't resist. He chases it, gets the bell, and immediately **squats down to "wash" it in the pond** (real raccoon behaviour).
  - Jarik pushes the **lily pad** that NUGGET is standing on.
  - NUGGET drifts to the middle of the pond still washing the bell, then realises where he is: "…uh-oh."
  - *SPLASH!* He falls in.
- **Jarik (taking the bell back):** "Finders keepers." *(Yang nemu, yang punya.)*

**Shot 20 — Duel 2: POM-POM vs JIMOTHY (the garden slope)**
- **Visual:**
  - Jimothy curls up and **rolls like a bowling ball** straight at Pom-Pom.
  - Pom-Pom panics, closes his eyes, and holds out his **blue bowl** in front of him.
  - Jimothy bounces off the bowl, rolls down the slope, and flops into the **wheelbarrow**.
  - Pom-Pom opens his eyes, sees his chance, and **pushes the wheelbarrow** straight down to the pond.
  - *SPLASH!*
- **Pom-Pom (shocked, then proud):** "I… I WON?!" *(Aku… MENANG?!)*
- **Caption:** `Pom-Pom's FIRST WIN in 8 episodes.`

**Shot 21 — Duel 3: GABAN vs GERALD (under the tree)**
- **Visual:**
  - GERALD smirks, cracks his long fingers, and **climbs the tree** to jump on GABAN from above.
  - GABAN turns out to be more agile and gets to the higher branch first.
  - **LION PUFF:** GABAN's ruff and tail fluff up to twice their size, so his silhouette covers the sun.
  - GERALD's toothpick drops, and he yawns out of fear and sleepiness.
  - **TAIL SWEEP:** GABAN sweeps his giant tail, and GERALD slides off the branch.
  - *SPLASH!* He lands in the pond.

**Shot 22 — Three wet raccoons**
- **Visual:** the three raccoons surface in the pond with dripping fur, looking small and pitiful. NUGGET is still washing the spoon he stole. On the pond edge, GABAN (tail high), Pom-Pom and Jarik stand shoulder to shoulder.
- **SFX:** a fish jumps and slaps Jimothy's face.

- **Visual:** the owner (the young woman with glasses and a curly bun) opens the terrace door, still half asleep with a mug of coffee. She sees three animals sitting neatly and innocently in the garden: GABAN, Pom-Pom and Jarik (Jarik quickly hides behind GABAN's tail).

## Key points

- **3 VS 3 match-ups** (don't swap them): **GABAN ↔ GERALD**, **POM-POM ↔ JIMOTHY**, **JARIK ↔ NUGGET**.
- **Every duel ends in the pond.** This is GABAN's plan: raccoons love water. In real life raccoons really do dunk their food in water.
- **The sunrise** has two jobs: it gives GABAN his entrance (golden backlight) and it's the raccoons' weakness (they're nocturnal and dazzled).
- **GABAN's voice:** one big yowl at his entrance (the territorial call). After that he only chirps and trills, and that contrast is the comedy.
- **Pom-Pom's first win** after 7 episodes of losing: the payoff of the series' running gag.
- **Assets that already exist:** night living room (`08690b95-…`), day living room (`7962058d-…`), and the sheets for all characters (see `characters/`). **New assets needed:** the terrace with steps to the garden, and the garden with a fish pond, a tree, a slope, a wheelbarrow, a fence with a gap, and an east wall.
