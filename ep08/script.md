## Shot 1 — The gang arrives (0:00–0:05)
- **Visual:** just before dawn, with a blue sky and a low moon. **Three masked silhouettes** stand on the terrace wall: **O** Jimothy (the blue bowl from EP07 on his head), **I** GERALD, **o** NUGGET. GERALD opens the pet flap lock with his paperclip, *click!*
- **Caption:** `He lost in EP07... so he brought FRIENDS.`

## Shot 2 — The raid (0:05–0:11)
- **Visual:** a fast montage in the dark living room:
  - Jimothy opens the fridge.
  - NUGGET stuffs cookies into his red backpack.
  - GERALD lounges on the sofa.
- **Jimothy (flat, mouth full):** "Revenge… and snacks." *(Balas dendam… dan camilan.)*

## Shot 3 — Trapped (0:11–0:17)
- **Visual:**
  - Pom-Pom wakes up and Jarik puts on his thimble helmet.
  - Pom-Pom charges and **bounces off** Jimothy's belly.
  - The three raccoons slowly close in, and Pom-Pom and Jarik back into a corner.
- **Caption:** `2 vs 3... TRAPPED.`

## Shot 4 — The call to GABAN (0:17–0:23)
- **Visual:** Pom-Pom hides behind the sofa, holding his phone. The screen reads **"GABAN 🦁⚓"**, and a small photo on the shelf shows kitten Pom-Pom next to a kitten GABAN.
- **Pom-Pom:** "GABAN… I need you. NOW." *(GABAN… aku butuh kamu. SEKARANG.)*
- **From the phone:** *"Brrrp."* The call ends.

## Shot 5 — The silhouette at sunrise (0:23–0:29)
- **Visual:** the sun rises in the east. On top of the garden wall, directly in front of the sun, stands the **silhouette of a huge cat**. His plume tail rises slowly until it **towers above his ears**, and the edge of his fur glows gold.
- **SFX:** a deep, echoing yowl, like a roar: *"MRRROOOWWRR!"*
- **Caption:** `THIS IS MY TERRITORY NOW.`

## Shot 6 — GABAN walks in, the raccoons are dazzled (0:29–0:35)
- **Visual:**
  - GABAN jumps down and walks towards the camera in slow motion, head low and tail swaying high (recreating the reference video).
  - The three raccoons, who have fled into the garden with their loot, squint at the sun because they are nocturnal.
  - Inside, Pom-Pom and Jarik press their faces to the glass, relieved.
- **Pom-Pom:** "GABAAAN!"

## Shot 7 — The plan (0:35–0:40)
- **Visual:** GABAN, Pom-Pom and Jarik huddle behind a bush. GABAN scratches a circle (the pond) and three arrows into the soil with his claw, and the three of them put their paws together.
- **GABAN (calm, Maine accent):** "Raccoons love water. Pond. Together. Ayuh." *(Rakun suka air. Ke kolam. Bareng-bareng. Iya.)*
- **SFX:** *"Mrrt!"* (the attack signal).

## Shot 8 — Face-off 3 VS 3 (0:40–0:45)
- **Visual:** a wide shot of the lawn next to the pond, with the golden sun behind the heroes. **Left:** GABAN, Pom-Pom, Jarik. **Right:** GERALD, Jimothy, NUGGET. They face each other in matching pairs: **GABAN ↔ GERALD, POM-POM ↔ JIMOTHY, JARIK ↔ NUGGET**. Leaves blow between them.
- **Caption:** `3 VS 3`

## Shot 9 — Team attack: one combo into the pond (0:45–0:53)
One continuous team action, so the teamwork shows (not 1 vs 1):
- **Visual:**
  1. Jarik rolls the shiny gold bell towards the pond edge. All three raccoons chase it (they can't resist shiny things).
  2. Pom-Pom holds up his blue bowl like a shield and blocks them from behind, so the three raccoons **bunch up** at the pond edge.
  3. GABAN does a **LION PUFF**: his ruff and tail fluff up until his silhouette covers the sun.
  4. He finishes with a **TAIL SWEEP**: one sweep of his giant tail sends all three raccoons flying.
  5. **SPLASH!** All three raccoons fall into the pond together.
- **Visual:** the three heroes land side by side at the pond edge in a hero pose.
- **Caption:** `TEAMWORK.`

## Shot 10 — Victory (0:53–1:00)
- **Visual:**
  - The three soaked raccoons climb out and run for the fence. Jimothy gets stuck in the gap in the fence (*POP!*), and NUGGET's backpack of stolen food is left behind.
  - GABAN wraps his tail around Pom-Pom and Jarik and gives a tiny *"brrrp"*.
  - Pom-Pom says: "We… WON?!" *(Kita… MENANG?!)*
- **Score overlay (editing):** a scoreboard pops up with a "ding!": `POM-POM +1` · `JARIK +1`, because this is a win for both of them. This is the last raccoon revenge arc, so there is no teaser for a follow-up.
- **Caption:** `Follow @pompomjarik`

## Why it fits in 60 seconds and saves credits
- **10 shots** instead of 26, so the Higgsfield video budget drops by more than half.
- **The battle is only 2 shots** (the face-off and the team combo) instead of 3 separate 1 vs 1 duels.
- **The heist (1–2) and the trap (3–4) are compressed** into fast montages with captions.
- **Assets that already exist:** the night living room, and the sheets for all six characters. **New assets needed (minimum):** the garden with the pond and the east wall (one establishing image, reused for shots 5–10).
