## 1. Maine Coon facts the character is built on

| Fact about the real breed | How GABAN uses it |
|---|---|
| The only native American longhair breed, from the state of **Maine** (USA). It started as a farm and ship's cat and was valued as a tough mouser. It is Maine's official state cat. | Ship's-cat heritage → navy sailor neckerchief with an anchor charm. He is calm, practical and "has seen worse storms". |
| Built for Maine winters: a water-resistant double coat, a thick **ruff** (mane) around the neck, **"snowshoe" paws** with fur tufts between the toes, **lynx tips** on the ears, and a very long bushy tail that it wraps around itself like a scarf. | His "armour" and his tools (see *Moves* below). |
| **Big and slow to mature.** Males usually weigh 6–8 kg (13–18 lb), can reach about 1 m from nose to tail tip, and only reach full size at 3–5 years. Long rectangular body, muscular, **not fat**. | Silhouette contrast: Pom-Pom = round, Jimothy = a ball, GABAN = long and tall. |
| **"Gentle giant"** personality: friendly, patient, loyal like a dog, smart, playful all its life, rarely aggressive. | Doesn't pick fights. He ends them. |
| Usually **chirps and trills** instead of meowing. | Comedy: a huge cat with a tiny "brrrp?" voice. He also uses the chirp as a team signal. |
| Many love **water**: they dip a paw in the water bowl and play in the tap. | His ace: splashing and water traps. |
| Many old Maine Coons were **polydactyl** (extra toes). | Optional: 6 toes on the front paws = extra grip and a bigger "slap". |
| **The raccoon myth.** The name "Coon" comes from the folk legend that it is a cross between a cat and a raccoon, because of its bushy tail and brown tabby coat. **Biologically this is impossible**, since cats and raccoons are different families (Felidae vs Procyonidae). | Use it as a **legend or a joke**, not a fact. The three raccoons think GABAN is a "distant cousin" and try to recruit him, and he answers: *"Ayuh… that's just a myth. I'm a cat."* |

> Note for the script: the premise "GABAN still has a genetic line with the raccoons" can stay, but as **a story the characters believe** (a legend in Pom-Pom's head or the raccoons' misunderstanding), not as a real fact. That keeps it accurate and turns it into a good comedy beat.

## 2. Visual design (lock these for consistency)

- **Species / age / sex:** adult male Maine Coon, about 4 years old (fully grown).
- **Size in the series:** about 1.5× Pom-Pom's height when standing, and noticeably longer. Next to Jimothy he is taller and longer, but not as round.
- **Coat:** shaggy semi-long **brown classic tabby** with golden-ginger/russet tones. The legs and back have darker brown-grey stripes. Clear **"M" mark** on the forehead. The back legs have "britches" (fluffy pants).
- **Ruff:** a thick, fiery golden-ginger mane, like a small lion's.
- **Head:** slightly square muzzle, high cheekbones, long white whiskers, **amber-green eyes**.
- **Ears:** very tall, with **long black lynx tips** and fur tufts inside.
- **Paws:** oversized round "snowshoe" paws with tufts of fur between the toes.
- **Tail:** extremely long plume with faint rings, **always held high like a flag** (as in the reference video).
- **Signature accessory:** a **faded navy-blue sailor neckerchief** knotted at the side, with a **small brass anchor charm**. It matches the others: Pom-Pom has a red collar with a gold bell, Jarik has a green scarf, Jimothy has a cream crossbody pouch.
- **Walk:** slow, heavy and confident. Big paws land softly, the tail sways high, and the head is low and steady like a stalking lynx (see the reference video).

Coat note: the cat in the reference video has tortie/torbie-like patches (red and brown mixed). In real cats that pattern is almost always **female**. GABAN is male, so his design uses a **brown tabby with a golden ruff**. It looks close to the reference and is still genetically plausible.

## 3. Personality

- **Gentle giant.** Calm, patient, easy-going, and friendly to everyone, including Jarik (a small joke: Jarik panics at first because GABAN's ancestors were "professional mousers").
- **Loyal like a dog.** He comes as soon as Pom-Pom calls, and he follows his friends everywhere.
- **Wise and smart.** He reads the situation before acting and gives the long "slow blink" (a sign of trust in cats) before making a decision.
- **Playful.** He likes chasing leaves and playing with water, like a kitten stuck in a giant body.
- **Protective.** Only when a friend is threatened does he "switch on": the ruff and tail fluff up, the ears go forward, and he stands up tall.
- **Voice:** tiny chirps and trills (*"brrrp?"*, *"mrrrt!"*). He doesn't meow and never hisses unless it is serious. If he gets dialogue, give it a Maine accent: *"Ayuh"* (= yes).

## 4. Moves vs the 3 raccoons (based on real traits)

1. **Tail Sweep.** The giant plume tail sweeps like a broom and knocks over whatever a raccoon is holding.
2. **Snowshoe Stomp.** His big paws don't slip on the parquet. He stomps a raccoon's tail and holds it in place.
3. **Splash Trap.** Raccoons love to "wash" their food, and GABAN loves water too. He uses water as bait and as a weapon.
4. **Lion Puff.** The ruff and tail fluff up and his silhouette doubles in size. Pure intimidation.
5. **Chirp Signal.** A code between Pom-Pom, Jarik and GABAN: one chirp = attack, two trills = retreat.
6. **Bookshelf Climb.** He is agile and a good climber despite his size. He gets onto the top of a shelf and drops straight onto them.

## 5. EP08 beat suggestions (draft)

1. **Cold open:** at midnight on the balcony, Jimothy (still with the blue bowl on his head?) comes back with **2 new raccoon friends**. Three masked silhouettes appear against the moonlight.
2. The raccoons raid the living room. Pom-Pom & Jarik are cornered, 3 vs 2.
3. Pom-Pom has an idea: he types on his phone → *"GABAN, I need you."*
4. **GABAN's entrance:** the pet flap bulges, with shots of his big paws first, the tall tail, and a slow-motion walk (recreating the reference video). The raccoons freeze.
5. **Myth gag:** the raccoons see his tail and brown fur: "Cousin?!" They offer him a pie. GABAN slow-blinks: *"brrrp… nope."*
6. **Team battle:** Tail Sweep, Snowshoe Stomp and the Splash Trap, with Jarik as the tactician and Pom-Pom as the bait (and, as usual, the victim).
7. **Ending:** the three raccoons flee over the balcony railing, soaking wet. GABAN curls up on the sofa and wraps his tail around Pom-Pom and Jarik like a blanket. The last joke: GABAN dips his paw in Pom-Pom's bowl.
