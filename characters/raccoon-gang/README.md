# Jimothy's Raccoon Gang (EP08)

In EP08 Jimothy comes back after losing in EP07, and this time he brings two friends. All three are funny, nonchalant, naughty, love stealing and love bothering Pom-Pom & Jarik. They never look angry, just **unbothered** while they wreck the house.

Three silhouettes you can tell apart from far away: **O – I – o**.

## Real raccoon behaviour behind their comedy

- **Very dexterous hands**, with 5 sensitive fingers. Real raccoons can open latches, lids, jars and even simple locks. → GERALD the lock-picker.
- **"Washing" food.** Raccoons often dunk and rub food in water, partly because wet paws make their sense of touch sharper. → NUGGET obsessively "washes" every stolen item. Weak point: a **water puddle distracts him instantly**, which links to GABAN's Splash Trap.
- **Opportunistic omnivores.** They eat anything and raid bins and kitchens ("trash pandas"). → They loot the fridge and the snack stash.
- **Nocturnal.** → The raid happens at midnight, like EP07.
- **Hard to deter.** They keep coming back to the same place. → A good reason for Jimothy's return in EP08.

## GERALD

- **Physical:** a tall, skinny, slouching adult raccoon with long arms and legs, very long fingers, and a long thin ringed tail that drags lazily.
- **Personality:** the laziest and most unbothered of the three. He barely moves, but whatever lock he touches opens. He rolls his eyes a lot, and the toothpick flips from one side of his mouth to the other.
- **Signature gag:** he casually opens anything, like the fridge, a jar or the snack drawer, while looking the other way, then whistles innocently.
- **Weakness:** too lazy to run. He is always last to escape.

## NUGGET

- **Physical:** a small young raccoon with a big head and round ears, huge shiny eyes, silvery-grey fur, and a short tail that twitches nonstop.
- **Personality:** a small gremlin who looks cute but steals anything shiny (spoons, bottle caps, socks, Pom-Pom's gold bell?). When he gets caught he puts on big "innocent" eyes while hiding the loot behind his back.
- **Signature gag:** he steals something, **washes it** with a serious face in any water he can find, then stuffs it into his backpack. He blows a raspberry at Pom-Pom.
- **Weakness:** he can't resist water. The Splash Trap works best on him.

## Ideas for EP08

- **Opening:** three silhouettes on the balcony in the moonlight, **O – I – o**. GERALD opens the pet flap lock with his paperclip in one second.
- **Myth gag:** NUGGET is the first to see GABAN's giant ringed tail. *"Cousin?!"* He offers GABAN a stolen cookie.
- **Payoff:** NUGGET gets distracted by the Splash Trap, GERALD is too lazy to run, and Jimothy gets stuck in the pet flap because he is too round.
